🐟 Feeding Practices

Good feeding practices support healthy fish growth. Choose suitable feed for the species and growth stage you are raising.

  • Use feed appropriate for your fish species.
  • Follow the feed manufacturer's guidance.
  • Observe feeding behaviour and adjust as needed.
  • Remove uneaten feed where practical.

💧 Water Quality

Water quality affects fish health, feeding, and survival. Monitor your pond or tank conditions regularly.

  • Monitor temperature and dissolved oxygen.
  • Check pH and other relevant water parameters.
  • Watch for unusual changes in fish behaviour.
  • Maintain suitable water conditions for your species.

📋 Fish Farm Management

Keeping accurate records helps you understand how your farm is performing.

  • Record the number of fish stocked.
  • Track feed purchases and feeding activities.
  • Record fish deaths and unusual observations.
  • Review growth and farm expenses regularly.
